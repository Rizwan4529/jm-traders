About Us

In brief

Welcome to Jungle Jelly
Lebanon’s trusted source for natural, delicious jelly snacks made with love.

From our small village of Kfarhabou, Zgharta, to tables across Lebanon and beyond, we craft joy-filled treats that everyone can enjoy — fruit-flavored jelly cups and refreshing jelly drinks made with 100% natural, plant-based ingredients.

🍓
Free from animal gelatin, pork fats, and GMOs — just pure, tasty goodness.

🏆
Manufactured in an FDA-certified facility, ensuring the highest standards of quality and safety.

🌍
Proudly exporting to Germany, the Netherlands, the UK, UAE, the US, Australia, and more.

Discover our flavors and experience the refreshing taste of Jungle Jelly today!
